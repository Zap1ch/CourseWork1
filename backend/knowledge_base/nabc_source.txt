THE NABC VALUE PROPOSITION METHODOLOGY
Source: Carlson, C. R., & Polizzotto, L. (2019). "The ‘NABC’s’ of Value Propositions". IEEE Engineering Management Review.

1. NEED (N) - Ринокова Потреба
The most critical step is defining the customer's need. A need must be quantitative and specific. It is not enough to say "people want to be healthy." A valid need sounds like: "There is a 40% increase in crop loss due to undetected pests, costing farmers $5B annually."
Key questions for Need:
- Who is the specific customer?
- What is the specific pain point?
- How large is the market opportunity?
- Is the need urgent and growing?

2. APPROACH (A) - Підхід та Рішення
The Approach includes both the technical solution and the business model. It must be unique and defensible (e.g., patents, unique expertise).
Key questions for Approach:
- What is the technical solution? (How does it work?)
- What is the business model? (How will it make money?)
- How is this approach different from standard solutions?
- Is the solution scalable?

3. BENEFIT (B) - Вигоди на витрати (Benefits per Costs)
This is the value proposition. The benefits must be significantly higher than the costs (at least 2x-10x better). Benefits can be financial, environmental, or social. Costs include not just price, but time, effort, and risk.
Key questions for Benefit:
- What are the specific quantitative benefits? (e.g., "saves 20 hours per week", "reduces cost by 50%").
- Why is this value proposition superior to the status quo?
- What is the ROI (Return on Investment) for the user?

4. COMPETITION (C) - Конкуренція та Альтернативи
Competition is not just other companies; it is also the "status quo" (doing nothing). You must explain why your Approach is better than all alternatives.
Key questions for Competition:
- Who are the direct competitors?
- What are the alternative ways to solve this problem?
- Why is your solution better? (Cheaper, faster, more accurate?).
- What are the barriers to entry for others?
