COMMON REASONS WHY STARTUPS FAIL
Типові причини провалу стартапів

1. NO REAL NEED
The most common reason is building a product that nobody needs strongly enough. The founders like the solution and look for a problem afterwards. Warning signs: the problem was not discussed with real customers, the customers say "interesting" but do not ask when they can use it, the problem is a small inconvenience and not a pain.

2. RUNNING OUT OF MONEY AND TIME
The team builds a large product for many months before showing it to anyone. When it turns out that changes are needed, there is no money or motivation left. A small first version tested early is cheaper than a perfect product nobody tried.

3. WEAK OR MISSING BUSINESS MODEL
People use the product but nobody pays, or it costs more to attract a customer than the customer brings. Popularity without income does not last.

4. STRONGER COMPETITION
A competitor with more money, more users or a better product solves the same problem. A large company can add the same function for free to a product people already use. An idea without any barrier against copying is exposed to this risk.

5. WRONG TEAM FOR THE TASK
The team lacks a key skill: nobody can build the technology, or nobody can sell. For technical ideas that need special data, hardware or licences, the lack of access to them stops the project.

6. THE CHICKEN AND EGG PROBLEM
Products that connect two sides, such as buyers and sellers or students and tutors, are useless until both sides are present. Without a plan for how to attract the first side, the platform stays empty.

7. DEPENDENCE ON OTHERS
The product depends completely on one platform, one data source or one partner: a social network, a university system, a paid AI service. If the partner changes prices, rules or access, the product stops working.

8. LEGAL AND TRUST BARRIERS
Ideas in medicine, finance, education of children and personal data need permissions, certification and user trust. Ignoring this at the start leads to a product that cannot be launched legally.

9. HOW TO USE THIS LIST IN VALIDATION
For every idea ask which of these reasons is the most likely for it, and name it as the main risk. A good analysis does not say that the idea will fail. It says what must be true for the idea to avoid the typical failure, and how to check it early.
